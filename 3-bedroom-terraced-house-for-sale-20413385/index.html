Description

A beautifully presented and characterful three-bedroom period home, offering accommodation arranged over three floors, a superb extended dining kitchen and attractive gardens plus off-street parking space, situated in a convenient position on the northern side of Harrogate.

The property has been thoughtfully improved by the current owners, successfully combining original character with a stylish contemporary finish. Extending to approximately 927 sq ft, the accommodation is deceptively spacious and includes some particularly attractive features including exposed timber flooring, feature fireplaces and a wood-burning stove.

The ground floor opens into a welcoming sitting room with a large window providing plenty of natural light and an attractive brick fireplace housing the wood-burning stove. To the rear is the real heart of the house, a superb extended dining kitchen which has been completely transformed to create a stylish and highly practical entertaining space. The kitchen is fitted with a range of contemporary dark green units, integrated appliances and timber-effect work surfaces, while a rooflight brings additional natural light into the rear extension. There is ample space for a dining table and glazed doors provide direct access outside.

On the first floor is a generous principal bedroom with fitted wardrobes and attractive timber flooring, together with a well-appointed house bathroom. Two further bedrooms occupy the second floor, both benefiting from the character of the roofline, with one featuring a Velux window and exposed beam. There is also the considerable advantage of a separate WC on this level.

Outside to the rear there is a lovely, extremely private south-facing flagged-patio courtyard garden ideal for entertaining and enjoying the sun throughout the day. The rear gate provides access on to one dedicated parking space - a rarity in houses of this type.

EPC Rating: D
